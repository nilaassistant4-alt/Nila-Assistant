# ADR-0004 — Shrunk whitening and dual-space scoring for voiceprint

**Status:** Accepted · **Supersedes:** (a) raw cosine over L2-normalised MFCC
statistics, (b) full PCA whitening

## Context

Speaker verification must separate one enrolled voice from every other voice,
with no GPU, no `torch`, and no model download. Two earlier approaches failed:

1. **Raw cosine over L2-normalised MFCC mean/std** scored 0.959–0.987 *across
   different speakers*. Completely unusable — the feature space was dominated by
   channel and recording-condition variance common to everyone.
2. **Full PCA whitening** collapsed impostor scores. Root cause: the covariance
   matrix is **rank-deficient** (fewer calibration samples than dimensions), so
   the smallest eigenvalues are numerical noise. Whitening divides by
   `sqrt(λ_c)`, amplifying that noise by ~1000×.

Also rejected: whitening-only (`alpha=0`), `keep=86`, per-user adaptive
thresholds (FNR 15–17%), and evaluating embedding-vs-embedding instead of
simulating real enrolment.

## Decision

**Shrink the whitening** so no direction can be amplified beyond a bounded
factor, and **score in two spaces** so the decision does not rest on one view.

```
whitening:  1 / sqrt(max(λ_c, floor · λ_max)),  floor = 0.1,  ridge = 1e-6
projection: keep = 40 of 86 raw dimensions
blend(s)  = 0.75 · cos(candidate, centroid) + 0.25 · max_i cos(candidate, template_i)
score     = alpha · blend(z-space) + (1 - alpha) · blend(w-space),   alpha = 0.75
```

Thresholds are **measured, not chosen**: `scripts/calibrate_voice_model.py` runs
36 dev + 24 eval synthetic speakers × 6 utterances (3 for enrolment), sweeps
thresholds, and ships the equal-error-rate point.

| Preset | Threshold | FAR | FRR |
|---|---|---|---|
| balanced (**shipped**) | 0.6199 | 0.36% | 0.0% |
| secure | 0.5742 | 1.03% | 0.0% |
| convenient | 0.4425 | 5.01% | — |

Genuine mean 0.8618 (min 0.6285); impostor mean 0.0257 (max 0.7574, n=1656).
**EER 0.18%.**

## Consequences

**Gained:** zero false accepts across 11 held-out speakers in testing; a
calibration path that produces a defensible threshold instead of a guessed one;
scoring that degrades gracefully if one space is noisy.

**Paid — and this is the important part:** **the EER is measured on synthetic
speakers** generated by `scripts/synth_voice.py`. It proves the pipeline, the
calibration methodology and the *relative* separation of genuine from impostor.
**It is not a real-world EER claim.** The model file carries that warning
inline, and `THREAT_MODEL.md` records it as residual risk R-1. Before trusting
Nila with real voices, recalibrate against a genuine corpus.

The 86-dim feature vector also depends on the audio backend; rule **P-9** makes
a backend mismatch raise rather than silently score garbage against an
incompatible template.

**Rejected:** neural speaker embeddings (`torch`/`resemblyzer`) — violates
ADR-0001; GMM-UBM — needs far more calibration data than a 3-utterance
enrolment provides; x-vectors — same dependency problem.
